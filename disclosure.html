Our role

We act solely as a distributor of mutual fund schemes. Our activity is limited to marketing, sale and distribution of the mutual fund products we offer, and to providing service and assistance that is incidental to that activity.

We are not registered with SEBI as an Investment Adviser and we do not provide investment advisory or portfolio management services. Any information or assistance we offer is incidental to distribution and should not be construed as investment advice. For advice, please consult a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser.

Regular Plans only

In terms of SEBI's directions, mutual fund distributors cannot deal in Direct Plans. Any transaction executed through us is in the Regular Plan of the scheme, which carries a distributor commission built into the scheme's expense ratio.

Direct Plans of the same scheme are available to you directly from the Asset Management Company and carry a lower expense ratio because they do not include distributor commission. You are free to invest in Direct Plans without our involvement.

How we are remunerated

We receive commission from the Asset Management Companies whose schemes we distribute. We do not charge you any separate fee for mutual fund distribution. Commission rates differ between schemes and between AMCs. We will disclose all material information, including commissions received or receivable, for the competing schemes from among which a scheme is presented to you.

Conflict of interest

Because commission rates vary across competing schemes, a potential conflict of interest exists. We disclose this openly, and we do not allow financial incentive to form the basis of any scheme or transaction we present to you.

Where we are an associate or group company of any AMC, or have any other material association, we will disclose that association and the total commission received or receivable. (Divine Partners is not an associate, group company or sponsor of any Asset Management Company.)

The information we provide is limited to the mutual fund products we distribute. Other alternative products, which we do not offer, may also be available to you and may be worth considering before you decide.

What we will not do

  • We will not indicate or assure any return or yield for any scheme or transaction.
  • We will not rebate or pass back commission, or induce investment through gifts or gift vouchers.
  • We will not conceal or omit any material fact or risk factor of a scheme.
  • We will not make any misrepresentation or exaggerated statement about any scheme, AMC or other distributor.
  • We will not use the name, logo or mark of any AMC without that AMC's prior written approval.

Products other than mutual funds

Where we facilitate any product other than mutual funds (such as insurance), that product is governed by its own regulator, terms and disclosures. Such products are not mutual funds, and mutual fund disclosures do not apply to them. We will disclose our role and remuneration for those products separately.

Grievance redressal

If you have a complaint arising out of our marketing, sale or distribution activity, please write to info@divinepartners.in or call +91 99999 99999. We endeavour to resolve grievances promptly.

If your complaint relates to a mutual fund scheme and is not resolved to your satisfaction, you may approach the concerned Asset Management Company, or lodge a complaint on SEBI's SCORES platform. You may also refer to AMFI for distributor-related matters.
